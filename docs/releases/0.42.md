# 0.42 - League Arena, browser analysis phases and progression refinements

`0.42` is the stable milestone preview that follows `0.41.1`. It adds the
authenticated **League Arena** with seasonal matchmaking, authoritative result
settlement and administrative recovery; randomised player-tournament seeding with
scheduled and rescheduled starts; role-specific win rates, simplified achievement
cards and manual portrait unlocking; a permanent **Online Play** navigation entry;
and the merged browser-side neural MCTS research phases 2 through 3C-B as an
opt-in, non-production spike. It also adopts the **PolyForm Noncommercial License
1.0.0** and adds a voluntary support link.

## League Arena

The authenticated Arena gains a League workspace at `/leagues` for human, rated
online play. Leagues are additive: single-player games, casual player-tournament
legs, Elo policy and existing online behaviour are unchanged, and no AI opponent
can enter a queue.

Seasons and progression:

- Seasons are UTC calendar months with half-open boundaries; a game belongs to the
  season in which the server pairs it. Brandubh, Tablut and Copenhagen have
  separate standings and no cross-ruleset matchmaking.
- League points are win **+3**, draw **+1**, loss **-1**, clamped at zero after each
  authoritative result in stable ledger sequence. Tiers are Midgard (0),
  Nidavellir (6), Alfheim (15), Asgard (30) and Valhalla (50); a loss or result
  correction can demote a player, and inactivity never subtracts points.
- Ranking requires three eligible results; tied sporting records share a rank and
  account ID only stabilises pagination. Empty or unqualified populations show no
  invented rank, opponent or percentile.
- Season rollover resets league points only. Elo, permanent achievements and
  portraits are untouched.

Queue and matchmaking:

- Only active, verified human accounts with normal play permission may queue, with
  at most one active League game across all rulesets. Queue entry is explicit and
  ruleset-aware, expires after ten minutes or at the season boundary, and is
  idempotent.
- The fixed League clock is 600 seconds per player with a 5-second increment. The
  rating window starts at 200 Elo and widens by 100 per full minute to 600; the
  maximum bound is never silently relaxed and no AI is substituted.
- Matching re-validates eligibility, ratings, queue age, active games and season
  under the write lock, so no self-pair, duplicate seat or wrong ruleset can commit.
- Admission is limited to ten requests per rolling minute and one hundred per UTC
  day per account; withdrawals have their own separate allowance of ten per
  rolling minute and one hundred per UTC day. Exact retries and superseded
  requests consume no new allowance.
- The worker scans bounded age-ordered pages per ruleset, keeps a fixed starter
  horizon inside a sweep so continuous arrivals cannot postpone wraparound, and
  continues expiry and eligibility reconciliation during administrative pause,
  disabled admission and clock pause. Deployment-gate contention defers work to a
  later cycle instead of failing.

Results, corrections and recovery:

- Each official game has one server-owned League provenance record; the normal
  online result transaction durably enqueues accounting and settlement needs no
  browser.
- A correction replays the affected player's contributions in authoritative
  sequence because the points floor is nonlinear. Voids withdraw a contribution;
  no client computes a winner or a rating change.
- Seasons and standings are provisional during the 24-hour grace period. A game
  finishing exactly at the grace boundary is late: it keeps its Elo result and
  contributes no league points.
- Administrators can inspect queue, worker and settlement health, pause
  matchmaking, cancel a stale entry by its original request UUID, and schedule an
  audited, version-checked season rebuild with a private reason. Queue, standings,
  settlement and audit state participate in compatible backup/restore.
- Browser recovery works from the canonical queue read: an unresolved join can be
  retried with its original UUID, ruleset and generation, or explicitly discarded.
  Delayed requests and sibling intents from the same generation are fenced, and
  fresh reads retire committed or superseded intents.
- Storage hardening in the same change scopes shared SQLite writer leases to the
  application lifecycle, clears callbacks on reuse and keeps online and tournament
  transactions bounded. League and profile reads use existing indexes and bounded
  pages.

The League policy, API and recovery contract is documented in
[Leagues](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.42/docs/LEAGUES.md), with
measurement and browser/restore evidence in
[League Arena validation](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.42/docs/validation/league-arena.md).

## Player tournaments

- **Randomised seeding**: creator, administrator and automatic scheduled starts now
  draw a uniformly random permutation of every registered eligible account with
  `secrets.SystemRandom().shuffle()` and persist consecutive seeds `1..N` inside
  the existing tournament-start transaction
  ([#120](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/120)). Seeds are never derived
  from registration order, account IDs, usernames, ratings or earlier results.
  Concurrent starts commit exactly one draw and one start audit; failed starts roll
  back seeds, matches, legs, games and the audit together; rebuilds, disputes,
  restarts and backup/restore preserve committed seeds. Manual seed selection,
  reordering and duplicate-seed validation were removed. The deprecated `seeds`
  request property is still accepted for older clients, validated only as a list of
  strings, and ignored.
- **Registration deadline removed**: older creation requests may still send
  `registration_deadline`; the server ignores its value and the deprecated response
  field is `null`. The verified creator or an administrator can reschedule with
  the reviewed version. A cup filled by the planned instant starts automatically; a
  missed start needs a new schedule or a manual start with at least two eligible
  entrants.
- **Synchronised rounds**: modern two-game cups persist a progression policy in
  schema 22 and start with synchronised progression, while already-started and
  historical single-game formats keep their independent progression. A rebuild
  reconciles attached games in feeder order across all rounds, keeps proven seats,
  clocks and results, retires displaced live games in the administrative
  transaction, and seats replacements when their round opens.
- Bracket, paged match and individual match reads share the same round projection
  (`global_round`, `waiting_for_round`, retained and return game state), with one
  batched read for legs and one current-round lookup. The registration bracket
  previews unassigned seeds at capacity and becomes the seeded bracket at start.
- UI: the start dialog explains that seeds are randomised at start and keeps the
  reviewed version, busy state and stale-version handling. Entrants show "Seed
  pending randomization" before the start, persisted seeds afterwards, and the
  neutral "Not seeded" label for tournaments cancelled before starting. The
  reschedule dialog converts to UTC, handles DST transitions and prevents duplicate
  submissions. English and German strings reuse the existing themes and controls.

## Statistics, achievements and portraits

- **Role win rates** ([#122](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/122)):
  each statistics ruleset card now shows overall, attacker and defender win rates
  beside the existing per-side table, outcome bar, Elo and AI-opponent breakdown.
  The values reuse aggregates already returned by the personal statistics API, so
  previously completed games appear without migration or replay of history.
  Draws count as completed games, ongoing games are excluded, cancelled and voided
  online games do not contribute, and a role with no eligible completed games
  renders as an em dash instead of a misleading `0%`. No aggregation, eligibility
  policy, API contract or schema changed.
- **Simplified achievement cards**: earned-tier and recorded-progress lines no
  longer repeat threshold or highest-achieved values. Descriptions, state, tier,
  progress, next milestone, rewards and score remain, and the underlying
  achievement data, eligibility, automatic claiming and sorting are unchanged.
- **Responsive correction**: at 320 px with the Large interface scale the
  statistics headline uses two columns with the third metric spanning the row, and
  the detailed metric cards use two columns, so long German labels and percentage
  values stay readable without clipping. Default and Compact mobile layouts,
  desktop, tablet and 4K layouts are unchanged.
- **Manual portrait unlocking**
  ([#123](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/123)): eligible Hall of
  Renown cards offer **Unlock portrait**. Achievements still award automatically;
  the claim itself is authenticated and idempotent, preserves existing
  entitlements and selections, and equipping remains a separate action. A
  confirmed claim reveals the loaded artwork through five staggered feathered
  masks over 2.4 seconds, followed by a 1.05-second gold frame and rune
  celebration. Reduced motion shows the artwork immediately.
- Progression notifications keep the milestone and points notices and add
  localised **New portraits ready to unlock** and **New portraits unlocked** counts.
  The first response for each account establishes a silent baseline.
- On browsers without Canvas 2D filters, a Canvas shadow feathers the same
  expanding mask; if neither blur method initialises, a confirmed claim shows the
  standard coloured artwork. Safari on Apple platforms and physical-device
  performance remain unverified.

## Browser-side analysis research (opt-in spike)

The browser-analysis work merged in this milestone is research code under
`spikes/browser_offline/` plus pure `hnefatafl/` modules. It adds **no production
route**, API, authorisation, database, CSP or bundle dependency, and no production
frontend analysis mode. Pages are served manually from the spike directory, all
assets are same-origin and digest-verified, and results are explicitly
non-authoritative. Competitive checkpoints, physical GPU/mobile hardware, sustained
memory and playing-strength evidence remain external gates.

- **Phase 2** ([#108](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/108)) runs the
  unchanged Python `mcts_context()` generator in a Pyodide search worker against a
  persistent ONNX Runtime Web inference worker, with no second rules or search
  engine. Deterministic Level A parity is 21/21 exact across Brandubh, Tablut and
  Copenhagen; model-backed Level B parity is 7/7 best moves per ruleset on the
  disposable smoke model. Ownership, cancellation, bounded inference drain, worker
  replacement, bfcache/navigation recovery, model identity, staged-source integrity
  and an experimental analysis UI (Jarl plus neural MCTS) are validated.
- **Phase 3A** ([#112](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/112)) transports
  only legal probabilities and the value instead of every policy logit. On the same
  smoke fixture and commit, medians over three repetitions (two for the
  512/1024 case) improved from 533.5 to
  707.2 simulations/s (Brandubh 128/256, +32.6 %), 501.5 to 665.5 (Brandubh
  512/1024, +32.7 %), 365.4 to 446.9 (Tablut, +22.3 %) and 220.9 to 412.9
  (Copenhagen, +86.9 %); 1,024 fixed Brandubh simulations took 1.50–1.66 s instead
  of 1.76–2.32 s. End-to-end full/compact search parity and test-hook isolation are
  covered by dedicated gates. These are tiny structural smoke-model numbers, not
  competitive-model or device claims.
- **Phase 3B** ([#115](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/115)) adds an
  opt-in direct `MessageChannel` transport, a bounded exact inference cache and
  opt-in independent-search batching. The relay stays the default because direct
  transport showed no reproducible gain; the cache improved simulations/s by
  +21.9 % (quick) and +47.6 % (strong) with ~81–82 % hit rates on repeated
  positions; batching showed no CPU-smoke advantage. Single-tree batching and
  subtree reuse remain deferred with recorded rationale.
- **Phase 3C-A** ([#118](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/118)) delivers a
  reusable validation platform: competitive-evidence orchestration, a tournament
  harness with real native and browser adapters and full move-prefix replay, a
  sustained-memory validator with explicit coverage and acceptance thresholds, a
  physical-device/GPU evidence validator, process-agent ownership and bounded
  cleanup, malformed-input hardening and executable owner runbooks.
- **Phase 3C-B** ([#121](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/121)) applies
  that platform to representative 32-channel/2-block smoke checkpoints, records
  structural measurements and states every remaining acceptance gate as blocked
  rather than inferred.

The canonical record is the
[browser analysis roadmap](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.42/docs/designs/browser-analysis-roadmap.md)
with the
[Phase 2](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.42/docs/validation/browser-analysis-phase2.md),
[Phase 3A](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.42/docs/validation/browser-analysis-phase3.md),
[Phase 3B](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.42/docs/validation/browser-analysis-phase3b.md),
[Phase 3C-A](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.42/docs/validation/browser-analysis-phase3c.md) and
[Phase 3C-B](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.42/docs/validation/browser-analysis-phase3c-b.md)
validation reports.

## Security and stability

- League authorisation is permission-gated, CSRF- and origin-checked, and bound to
  the session account: the Arena sends `X-League-Account` and the server rejects a
  replaced session before reading or writing League data. Administrative actions
  require the tournament/account permissions and record an append-only audit with
  reasons and optimistic versions.
- The added routes are purely additive: the API permission inventory gains 16
  grants and none is removed. Player-tournament creation and start reuse the
  existing `play` grant.
- Shared online, tournament and League SQLite transactions now share a bounded
  writer cache scoped to the application lifecycle; stale leases are invalidated on
  shutdown and database replacement, and reused writers clear callbacks.
- Queue admission, withdrawal, registration, login and public rate limits are
  enforced at UTC boundaries; duplicate and delayed requests are fenced by request
  UUID and generation.
- Browser research paths keep bounded requests, queues, caches and timeouts,
  digest-verified same-origin assets, explicit model/position identity, owner-bound
  cancellation and tensor cleanup. Direct transport and caching do not bypass
  validation.
- The locked OpenTelemetry API dependency was patched and `npm run audit:ci`
  reported zero affected packages after the lockfile change. The fail-closed
  dependency audit job remains required.

## Compatibility and migration notes

- **Online schema 21 → 23.** An update from `0.41.1` applies the existing additive
  migrations for the persisted tournament progression policy (22) and the League
  authority tables (23). Account schema stays **9**, checkpoint tensor format **1**,
  job-archive schema **3**, and training configuration, remote-worker protocol and
  model files are unchanged. The migration is forward-only: back up with stopped
  writers, keep the matching `0.41.1` code with the backup, and require matching
  schemas for a restore. Cross-schema automatic rollback remains unsupported and
  requires reviewed manual recovery.
- **Release identifier `0.42`.** This release uses the two-part identifier `0.42`
  verbatim as its Git tag, `pyproject.toml`/`package.json` version, notes filename
  and asset name (`hnefatafl-0.42.tar.gz`). The updater and the publish workflow now
  accept `X.Y` as well as `X.Y.Z` stable identifiers and order `X.Y` as `X.Y.0`;
  published tags are never renamed. A two-part package version is a canonical
  exact-match identity for remote workers, so workers must run the same `0.42`
  identifier. **An already installed `0.41.1` updater cannot discover a two-part
  tag**, so refresh the updater from the verified `0.42` archive before updating as
  described above; replaced `0.42` updaters handle two-part and three-part tags.
- **Player tournament API**: `POST /api/player-tournaments/{id}/start` accepts
  `{ "version": N }`; the deprecated `seeds` list is accepted but ignored, and
  unknown properties or malformed values still return `422`. `registration_deadline`
  is ignored on creation and `null` in responses. A new
  `POST /api/player-tournaments/{id}/reschedule` route is available to the verified
  creator or an administrator.
- **Portraits**: `POST /api/portraits/unlock` is a new authenticated, idempotent
  claim. Achievement awards remain automatic, and the achievement data still
  contains threshold and highest-achieved values for clients that use them.
- **Licence**: distributing or modifying the software now requires compliance with
  the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0; commercial use is restricted.
- No configuration variable is added or removed for League Arena, tournaments,
  statistics, portraits or licensing. Public spectator, registration and online
  admission settings keep their previous defaults and switches.

## Upgrade and installation

1. Refresh the installed updater from the verified `0.42` archive **before** asking
   it to update. The updater shipped by `0.41.1` only recognises three-part stable
   tags, so it reports "no newer release" for `0.42` and rejects
   `--version 0.42`. Download `hnefatafl-0.42.tar.gz`, verify GitHub's published
   SHA-256 digest, then install the new updater file and wrapper from that source:

   ```sh
   sha256sum hnefatafl-0.42.tar.gz
   install -m 644 hnefatafl/deployment.py /usr/local/lib/hnefatafl/deployment.py
   install -m 755 scripts/linux/update.sh /usr/local/bin/hnefatafl-update
   install -m 755 scripts/linux/backup.sh /usr/local/bin/hnefatafl-backup
   hnefatafl-update --check
   ```

   Running `bash scripts/linux/install.sh` from the same verified source performs
   the same refresh and then the normal update, preserving configuration,
   accounts and passwords. Either way only the updater code changes at this step;
   the application release is still activated by the updater below.
2. Pause remote claim loops and unmanaged writers, then take a stopped-writer
   backup of the data root and protected configuration. Keep the matching `0.41.1`
   code with that backup.
3. After stable publication and source-asset verification:

   ```sh
   hnefatafl-update --check
   hnefatafl-backup
   hnefatafl-update --version 0.42
   ```

   On Proxmox, prefix with `pct exec <CTID> --`. Use the attached
   `hnefatafl-0.42.tar.gz`, not GitHub's automatic source archive. The updater
   selects `0.42` as the newest stable release and verifies the asset's SHA-256
   digest before extraction. Fresh installations follow
   [Deployment](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.42/docs/DEPLOYMENT.md).
4. Confirm readiness after the update and check existing accounts, games, replay
   analysis, achievements, portraits, tournaments, training jobs, checkpoints and
   models. The online schema is 23 after an update from `0.41.1`; League standings
   start empty because no League games existed before this release.
5. Update remote workers to **0.42** before new assignments. Existing
   exact-version matching remains authoritative; no new worker capability flag is
   introduced.
6. Resume writers and claim loops after readiness and compatibility checks.

## Validation

Every merged pull request passed all five `Verify` jobs (`security`, `python`,
`node22`, `browser`, `deployment-cross-version`) on its final head. Feature-level
evidence is recorded in each pull request and in the linked validation reports;
release-preparation validation is recorded in the release pull request. Successful
`Verify` on the exact merged `main` commit is required before the annotated `0.42`
tag and stable publication, and the publication workflow re-verifies the tag,
project version, tagged commit CI and published asset digest.

The combined release is still a milestone preview: disposable backend, browser and
worker evidence does not replace target-host deployment, capacity and combined
acceptance testing. See
[Release readiness](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.42/docs/RELEASE_READINESS.md)
for the remaining gates.

## Limitations

- League Arena physical-device and multi-hour capacity behaviour was not measured;
  browser coverage uses Chromium desktop and mobile emulation, and League
  measurements describe one host and workload combination.
- Cross-schema automatic rollback remains intentionally unsupported; reviewed
  manual recovery is required when live and snapshot schemas differ.
- Browser neural MCTS remains an isolated opt-in spike: there is no production
  analysis route or execution mode, no competitive checkpoint validation, no
  verified physical GPU/WebGPU execution, no physical mobile or Safari/Firefox
