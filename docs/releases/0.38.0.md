# 0.38.0 - Account profiles and current player identities

This stable milestone adds a unified account menu, self-service profiles and
password changes, current account names across games/history, and optional public
profile details. It includes all changes merged to `main` since published
`0.37.0`: [#61](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/61),
[#62](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/62) and
[#63](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/pull/63), including their review fixes.

**Upgrade and privacy:** the database advances from **schema 13 to 15**. Deploy
backend and frontend together and take a stopped-writer backup first. Newly
submitted populated profile fields publish automatically; clearing them withdraws
publication. Saved legacy-private fields remain hidden until that specific field
is edited or explicitly confirmed. Full birth dates stay private; only completed
age is public. Older binaries cannot open schema 15, and automatic cross-schema
restore is refused. Checkpoint tensor format **1**, job-archive schema **3** and
training configuration remain unchanged. Update workers to **0.38.0** for the
existing exact-version assignment policy.

Full comparison: [`0.37.0...0.38.0`](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/compare/0.37.0...0.38.0).

## Account and UI features

### One account menu and My Profile (#61, #63)

- The header groups the existing English/German language selector beside a
  username menu with **My Profile** and **Log out**. `/profile` brings optional
  personal details, email verification, password changes and appearance settings
  together without changing login usernames or account ownership.
- The menu supports mouse/touch, outside dismissal, Escape, arrow keys, Home/End,
  Space/Enter and Tab navigation. Long desktop names remain accessible when
  truncated; compact headers use an account icon and show the complete wrapping
  username inside the dropdown. Anonymous account screens retain language choice.
- The profile adds optional display name, country, city and full date of birth.
  Country labels are localized from the supported ISO country/territory catalog.
  Empty/null values clear optional data; account ID, login name, email, role and
  verification status are not editable through these fields.
- Existing System/Light/Dark/Wood modes, board and piece themes and interface
  scale are now directly inside **My Profile → Appearance**. Language stays in the
  header. Both apply immediately and retain the existing browser-storage keys,
  startup theme behavior, defaults and blocked-storage fallbacks. They remain
  device preferences, including across logout; they do not synchronize to an
  account or another device.
- Profile-load/save errors preserve useful controls and entered edits for retry.
  Password and email actions remain independent of a profile retry. Duplicate
  submissions are prevented and requests are cancelled when leaving the page.
  The new surfaces and feedback include English/German translations and desktop/
  compact layouts.

See [account menu and profile](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.38.0/docs/PROFILE.md)
and [appearance controls](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.38.0/docs/PERSONALIZATION.md#color-tokens-and-boards).

### Optional details, automatic publication and exact age (#63)

- Populated optional fields submitted under the new policy are public; clearing a
  field removes its public value on the next fresh read. There is no permanent
  visibility section or set of sharing checkboxes.
- The detailed leaderboard adds eligible country, city and integer **Age**, while
  the compact Dashboard leaderboard keeps its four columns. Ranking, tie-breaking,
  filters, pagination and rating calculations are preserved.
- Age means completed years on the server's current **UTC date**, sampled once for
  the entries and the caller's own standing. February 29 birthdays advance on
  March 1 in a non-leap year. Missing, invalid or cleared full dates produce no
  age; raw birth dates and birth years do not appear in public game/history/
  leaderboard responses. A historical year alone never invents a birthday or age.
- Existing fields withheld by legacy privacy settings stay withheld after
  migration. Only affected inputs show a temporary **Publish saved … on save**
  action. Editing or confirming one field publishes only that field; unrelated
  edits and Save without changes cannot publish other saved private values.
- A retained legacy birth year is owner-only, read-only transition information.
  Entering or clearing a full date supersedes it. Dates must be complete ISO
  calendar dates from 1900-01-01 through the current UTC date; incomplete, invalid
  and future dates are rejected.

See [sharing and transition behavior](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.38.0/docs/PROFILE.md#personal-details-and-sharing)
and [age calculation](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.38.0/docs/PROFILE.md#exact-age-and-private-birth-dates).

## Gameplay, history and identity improvements

### Current names with canonical attribution (#61, #62, #63)

- Account-linked human seats show the account's **current eligible public name**
  in active/saved games, game lists, replays, online participants/analysis,
  standings, personal statistics, Hall history and model-usage previews. Editing
  or clearing a display name changes existing labels on their next read.
- Display names stay editable and non-unique. **Display Name (@username)**, or
  equivalent compact attribution, keeps the canonical login identity visible when
  it differs; matching names are not repeated. A name never conveys an account
  role, ownership or permission.
- New account-backed human seats store immutable account IDs and canonical
  usernames. Existing linked game JSON is projected at read time, without bulk
  rewrites or changes to moves, analysis, seeds, timestamps, results, game versions
  or optimistic move guards. Rating and statistical attribution continue to use
  stable account identities.
- Only authoritative links resolve: single-player human IDs must match the game
  owner, and online seats use their recorded account IDs. Matching a name never
  creates an association. Unlinked legacy humans and AI/model/tournament
  provenance keep their historical labels. Missing linked accounts use localized
  **Unassigned** rather than revealing a saved private name.
- Navigation and existing Dashboard/lobby/leaderboard/replay read cycles refresh
  identity after profile saves. Mounted single-player games also refresh on
  focus, visibility and reconnect, without a new continuous polling timer or a
  background response replacing newer board state. Other sessions see changes on
  their next normal read/focus; offline clients retain their last successful view.

### Hall search matches displayed player labels (#61, #62)

Administrators with the existing all-data permission can search literal substrings
of public display names, canonical usernames, `@username` and a copied complete
**Display Name (@username)** label. The input accepts the full 148-code-point
attributed-label bound, including Unicode paste without truncation. Current private
names and obsolete linked display-name snapshots are excluded from the public
search projection. Normal-user visibility, administrator-only player filtering,
counts, cursor context and pagination keep their existing authorization rules.
Identity lookup remains batched; no per-game/per-player database read loop is added.

See [identity and historical compatibility](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.38.0/docs/PROFILE.md#names-in-games-and-history)
and [Hall behavior](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.38.0/docs/DASHBOARD.md).

## Account security and input fixes

- **Change password** requires the current password and matching new-password
  confirmation. Success keeps the submitting session, revokes other sessions and
  invalidates pending reset tokens/mail in the same transaction. Invalid current
  passwords leave the session intact. Existing scrypt, durable per-account/IP and
  global hash budgets, CSRF/origin checks and deployment freeze protection remain.
- Profile shows the authenticated email and verification state. Unverified users
  can reuse verification resend; the Dashboard warning links to the focused
  `/profile#email-verification` section. Existing expiring/single-use tokens,
  generic acknowledgments, mail delivery and rate limits are reused.
- Verification observations share ordering and account/session guards with Auth.
  A late response cannot undo a newer successful verification observation or cross
  logout/account changes. Failed reads do not discard known identity; revoked or
  expired authentication still ends the session. Concurrent refresh invalidations
  coalesce instead of adding a second verification store or polling timer.
- Display names and cities use the same NFC-normalized, trimmed Unicode-code-point
  bounds in browser and backend (80 and 100 respectively). Valid non-BMP text is
  no longer silently truncated by UTF-16 HTML length limits. Unsafe C0/C1 and
  bidirectional formatting controls are rejected before trimming, while ordinary
  multilingual text, combining marks and emoji joiners remain supported.
- Profile changes, registration and password reset share the existing new-password
  rule: 12–256 code points and at least four distinct characters. Login/current
  password fields retain their compatible bounds. Passwords are never normalized
  or truncated. Localized validation does not echo rejected sensitive input.
- The three new profile routes are own-session operations for users and
  administrators. They have no target-account parameter or override for editing
  someone else's details. Strict field allowlists, bounded requests and
  transactional session/permission rechecks prevent privileged-field assignment.
  The route/permission documentation and audit include the new grants.

See [profile security and API](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.38.0/docs/PROFILE.md#account-security)
and [account security](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.38.0/docs/SECURITY.md).

## Migration and API compatibility

- Schema **13 → 15** adds profile account columns, private full birth dates and an
  internal one-time publication-exception list. Migration is additive,
  transactional and idempotent; account IDs, roles, hashes, verification,
  sessions, ownership, ratings and game records are preserved.
- Pre-profile saved names may use the canonical username until the saved display
  name is reconfirmed. When upgrading an intermediate schema-14 installation,
  populated fields whose sharing flags were false remain private and previously
  public fields stay public. Migration never fabricates consent or a birthday.
- The original clock migration keeps its `<13` threshold: applying the profile
  migrations does not re-enable a paused timeout gate.
- New endpoints are `GET /api/profile`, `PATCH /api/profile` and
  `POST /api/profile/password`. Profile PATCH accepts only explicitly supplied
  `display_name`, `country_code`, `city` and `date_of_birth`; omitted fields remain
  unchanged. Legacy sharing flags, birth-year writes, transition metadata and
  privileged fields are not writable. Public account-backed player projections
  add canonical `username` attribution where applicable.
- Deploy paired frontend/backend contracts. Automatic restore requires matching
  schema-15 account columns and retains live publication exceptions and dates.
  Older-schema backups require explicit manual reconciliation or migration of a
  disposable copy; cross-schema automatic root replacement is refused. Older
  code refuses schema 15, so rollback needs the matching stopped-writer backup.
- Training/model/checkpoint formats and schema-3 job transfer are unaffected.
  Existing worker exact-version checks still require workers to match `0.38.0`;
  no new worker capability or protocol migration is introduced.

## Upgrade and installation

1. Stop/cancel training and tournaments, pause remote claim loops and unmanaged
   CLI writers, and take a complete stopped-writer backup of the data root and
   protected configuration. Keep matching `0.37.0` code with that backup.
2. After the stable release and verified source asset are available:

   ```sh
   hnefatafl-update --check
   hnefatafl-backup
   hnefatafl-update --version 0.38.0
   ```

   On Proxmox, prefix these commands with `pct exec <CTID> --`. Fresh installs
   follow [Deployment](https://github.com/freeDom-/hnefatafl/blob/0.38.0/docs/DEPLOYMENT.md).
   Use attached `hnefatafl-0.38.0.tar.gz`, not GitHub's automatic source archive.
3. Start the updated backend and frontend together, confirm schema **15** and
   application readiness, and verify representative account/login, profile,
   leaderboard and existing-game views. Do not run `0.37.0` against the migrated
   database or rely on cross-schema automatic restore.
4. Review saved legacy-private profile values before publishing them. Newly
   edited populated fields are public; clearing a field withdraws it. Full dates
   remain private, but entering one publishes completed age in the detailed
   leaderboard.
5. Update remote workers to **0.38.0** before assigning new work. Resume operational
   writers/claim loops after readiness and account migration have been checked.

## Limitations

- Profile details can disclose personal information when populated; birth-date
  privacy does not make the derived age private. Legacy exceptions are temporary
  transition protections, not permanent per-field sharing preferences.
- Appearance/language remain browser preferences and do not sync across devices.
  Other sessions and disconnected clients see identity changes on a subsequent
  successful normal read or focus refresh.
- Browser coverage is Chromium desktop/phone emulation with synthetic mailbox
  delivery, not physical-device or live-SMTP evidence. Production database migration
  and target-host deployment were not performed by these tests.
- Existing dependency/test warnings, Vite's bundle-size advisory and
  hardware-dependent backend skips remain separate from passing functional checks.
