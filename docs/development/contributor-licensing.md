# Deferred contributor licensing proposal

[Licensing](../LICENSING.md) · [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Development](setup.md)

**Status: deferred proposal only. No CLA is active.** The maintainer does not
currently intend to commercially exploit Hnefatafl. Ordinary noncommercial
contributions do not need a CLA, DCO or sign-off, and this proposal grants no
additional rights now. It would require explicit maintainer approval and legal
review before activation. This is not legal advice.

## Current position

- No CLA, copyright assignment, DCO enforcement or sign-off requirement exists.
- Contributors retain copyright in their own contributions unless separately agreed.
- Contributions are inbound under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 only;
  external commercial exploitation remains restricted by that license.
- A DCO would confirm submission rights but would not grant the broader rights a
  future commercial dual-license would need. It is not required for the current
  noncommercial project.

## Deferred option: Harmony Individual CLA 1.0, Option Five

If the owner explicitly seeks broader rights, use the
[Harmony Individual Contributor License Agreement 1.0](https://harmonyagreements.org/agreements/)
rather than drafting a new agreement:

- **Outbound:** Option Five would allow licensing a contribution under other terms,
  including commercial/proprietary, while it remains available under the project's
  submission-date license.
- **Media:** Apply the same treatment to artwork/documentation unless a contributor
  contributes under a different compatible license.
- **Patent:** Retain the Harmony patent license.
- **Copyright:** Use the license agreement, not the Contributor Assignment
  Agreement; contributors retain copyright.
- **Entity contributions:** Use the matching Harmony Entity CLA or confirm employer
  authorization.
- **Attribution:** Preserve the Harmony CC BY 3.0 attribution and legal terms; fill
  only project-specific placeholders after legal review. Do not publish an unsigned
  draft as an active agreement.

## Activation requirements (not planned)

Any activation needs explicit owner approval and legal review. Record the approved
agreement at a stable versioned path, add an entity agreement if needed, update
CONTRIBUTING with signing instructions, add a CLA check only after testing it on a
non-production fork, preserve signatures/agreement versions, and require
re-signature for later terms. Do not silently apply new terms to earlier
contributions.
